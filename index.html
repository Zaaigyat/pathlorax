Prepare and improve

Job Guides

Learn the basics of preparing for job applications and developing your professional skills.

📄

Creating a Resume

A resume introduces your education, skills, experience, and accomplishments to an employer.

  • Use a clear and readable format.
  • Include relevant education and skills.
  • Describe school projects and volunteer experience honestly.
  • Check spelling and contact details.
🎤

Preparing for Interviews

Preparation can help you communicate your abilities and interests confidently.

  • Research the employer and role.
  • Practice common interview questions.
  • Give honest examples of your skills.
  • Ask relevant questions about the job.
💻

Digital Skills

Digital skills can help you study, communicate, organize information, and complete work tasks.

  • Practice word processing and spreadsheets.
  • Learn safe online communication.
  • Build projects to demonstrate your abilities.
  • Keep learning new tools and technologies.
🤝

Workplace Skills

Employers often value how people communicate, cooperate, and handle responsibilities.

  • Communicate clearly and respectfully.
  • Practice teamwork and problem-solving.
  • Manage time and meet deadlines.
  • Accept feedback and learn from mistakes.
🎓

Training and Education

Training can help you develop the knowledge and practical skills needed for different careers.

  • Explore courses related to your interests.
  • Check course eligibility and requirements.
  • Compare costs and available assistance.
  • Verify providers through official sources.
🔒

Online Job Safety

Protect yourself when searching and applying for jobs online.

  • Verify employers and job advertisements.
  • Be cautious of requests for upfront payments.
  • Never share passwords or verification codes.
  • Protect personal information and documents.

Sustainable Development Goals

SDG 8: Decent Work and Economic Growth

Promoting productive employment, decent work, and inclusive economic opportunities.

Decent Work and Economic Growth

Sustainable Development Goal 8 promotes sustained, inclusive, and sustainable economic growth, productive employment, and decent work for all.

How PathLorax Supports SDG 8

PathLorax supports the goal by helping users explore career interests, learn about job opportunities, and access resources for developing work-related skills. Providing reliable employment links can help users make more informed decisions about their future.

🧭

Career Awareness

Help users explore different occupations and identify interests that may guide their career decisions.

📈

Skills Development

Encourage users to build practical, digital, communication, and professional skills.

🌐

Access to Resources

Connect users with employment and training websites where they can check opportunities and requirements.

Important: PathLorax is an informational project. It does not guarantee employment, provide jobs directly, or replace official employment and training services.
